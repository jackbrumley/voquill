Voquill

Truly free & open-source offline dictation that types into any app. Voice-to-text runs locally so your data never leaves your device.

Install 

    
  

  









  Voquill turns your voice into text right where your cursor is. Use hold-to-talk or toggle mode to dictate, and it types into any app — emails, docs, browsers, editors. Free and open source with Windows and Linux support, it runs entirely on your device so your voice stays private.

Features

  
    
    
  • 
      Post-Processing & Polish
      Filler-word removal strips "uh", "umm", "hmm" instantly. Optional LLM post-processing (local or cloud) turns rough speech into clean, publication-ready text.
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Local & Private
      Runs entirely on-device with Whisper.cpp GPU acceleration. Voice data never leaves your computer.
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Flexible Transcription
      Local Whisper (CPU/GPU), Parakeet/sherpa-onnx, or any OpenAI-compatible API.
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Speaker Diarization
      Automatically detects and labels different speakers in live dictation and imported files.
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Audio File Import
      WAV, MP3, M4A, OGG, FLAC through the full pipeline.
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Native Platform Support
      Full Wayland (XDG Portals), X11, and Windows.
    
    • 
  






  

    
Truly Free

    
GNU AGPLv3 licensed and completely free to use. No subscriptions, no accounts, and no "Pro" tiers—just a powerful tool that belongs to everyone.

  

  

    
100% Private

    
Your voice stays on your device. Everything is processed locally by Whisper.cpp so your data stays yours. Cloud processing is available as an optional extra if you choose to use it.

  

  

    
Open Source

    
Voquill is truly free and open source: no subscriptions, no paywalls, no usage limits. Just a free tool anyone can use, inspect, and build. Full Wayland support via XDG Portals.
