Truly free & open-source offline dictation for Windows and Linux. Your voice never leaves your computer.

Three Core Workflows

  

    

      
1. Dictate

      
Speak → Text Everywhere

      
Press your global shortcut, speak, and text appears directly at your cursor in any application—web browsers, email, word processors, notes, or messaging apps.

    

    

      
2. Transcribe

      
Audio → Transcript & Diarization

      
Import voice recordings (WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC). Automatically identify different speakers (`Speaker 1`, `Speaker 2`) and review transcripts with built-in audio playback.

    

    

      
3. Refine

      
Speech → Finished Writing

      
Instant filler-word removal plus built-in local AI post-processing. Transforms rough spoken thoughts into clean emails, formatted notes, or custom writing styles—100% offline without sending text to any cloud service.

    

  






  
Full Platform Capabilities

  
    
    
  • 
      On-Device AI Post-Processing & Polish
      Strips filler words ("uh", "um") instantly and runs local AI models directly on your computer to fix grammar, reformat notes, or rephrase dictation into polished prose—all 100% offline.
    
    • 
    
  • 
      100% Private & Offline
      Speech recognition runs directly on your computer. Your voice stays private on your device, with no account or signup required.
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Works in Any Application
      Type directly into any email, document, browser, or chat window on Windows and Linux with customizable hotkeys.
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Differentiate Voices
      Automatically identifies and labels different speakers (`Speaker 1`, `Speaker 2`) in meetings and voice recordings.
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Transcribe Audio Files
      Drag and drop recorded voice notes, interviews, and meetings (WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC) to convert them into accurate written text.
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Searchable Recording History
      Search through past dictations instantly and listen back to original voice recordings anytime with native audio playback.

Truly Free

    
GNU AGPLv3 licensed. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no accounts, and no "Pro" tiers—just a powerful open-source tool for everyone.

  

  

    
100% Private & Local

    
Private by default. On-device speech recognition powered by Whisper.cpp & Parakeet with zero telemetry. Optional cloud APIs if you choose.

  

  

    
Native & Cross-Platform

    
First-class Linux support (Wayland XDG Portals, X11, .deb, .rpm, AppImage) and Windows WASAPI / CoreAudio. Native keyboard simulation.
