What it does

    
Voquill turns your voice into text right where your cursor is. Hold a key to talk, or toggle, and it types into any app: emails, docs, browsers, editors. It's a free, open-source alternative to paid cloud dictation: everything runs on your own computer, so your voice never leaves it.

    
    
      
  • Works in any app: emails, documents, browsers, editors and chat, with a global hotkey

Three Core Workflows

    

      

        
1. Dictate

        
Speak → Text Everywhere

        
Press your global shortcut, speak, and text appears directly at your cursor in any application—web browsers, email, word processors, notes, or messaging apps.

      

      

        
2. Transcribe

        
Audio → Transcript & Diarization

        
Import voice recordings (WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC). Automatically identify different speakers (`Speaker 1`, `Speaker 2`) and review transcripts with built-in audio playback.

      

      

        
3. Refine

        
Speech → Finished Writing

        
Instant filler-word removal plus built-in local AI post-processing. Transforms rough spoken thoughts into clean emails, formatted notes, or custom writing styles—100% offline without sending text to any cloud service.

      

    

  


  

    
Full Platform Capabilities

    
    
      
  • 
        Hands-Free Voice Macros & Key Automation
        Trigger multi-step key sequences, app shortcuts, and native system commands hands-free using spoken trigger words with Voice Activation Thresholds.
      
    • 
      
  • 
        Local AI Voice Feedback & Voice Studio
        Instant spoken confirmations powered by on-device neural TTS with authentic tactical radio, cockpit AI, and sci-fi acoustic effects.
      
    • 
      
  • 
        On-Device AI & Local LLM Post-Processing
        Runs full AI language models (LLMs like Qwen & Llama) directly on your computer to fix grammar, reformat notes, or rephrase rough dictation into clean text—100% offline with zero cloud fees.
      
    • 
      
  • 
        Custom AI Prompts & Style Presets
        Choose or create custom AI prompts to shape your voice output: switch between professional emails, structured notes, or create custom writing styles.
      
    • 
      
  • 
        Custom Dictionary & Technical Words
        Add unique names, jargon, acronyms, and specialized terminology to your personal dictionary so names and technical terms are recognized more reliably.
      
    • 
      
  • 
        Instant Filler-Word Stripping
        Instant deterministic removal strips "uh", "um", "like", and "you know" before text reaches your cursor, without needing a full AI model pass.
      
    • 
      
  • 
        100% Private & Offline
        Speech recognition and AI models run locally on your computer. Your voice and text stay private on your device with no accounts or telemetry.
      
    • 
      
  • 
        Works in Any Application
        Type directly into any email, document, browser, editor, or chat window on Windows and Linux using customizable global hotkeys.
      
    • 
      
  • 
        Differentiate Voices & Transcribe Files
        Detect and label distinct speakers (`Speaker 1`, `Speaker 2`) in live dictations or drag-and-drop audio files (WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC, OGG).
      
    • 
      
  • 
        Searchable Recording History & Playback
        Search past dictations instantly with full-text search, listen back to original voice recordings with built-in audio playback, and compare raw vs. AI-cleaned text.

Truly Free

      
GNU AGPLv3 licensed. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no accounts, and no "Pro" tiers—just a powerful open-source tool for everyone.

    

    

      
100% Private & Local

      
Private by default. On-device speech recognition powered by Whisper.cpp & Parakeet with zero telemetry. Optional cloud APIs if you choose.

    

    

      
Native & Cross-Platform

      
First-class Linux support (Wayland XDG Portals, X11, .deb, .rpm, AppImage) and native Windows WASAPI audio. Native keyboard simulation.
